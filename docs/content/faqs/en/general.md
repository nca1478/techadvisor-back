# General frequently asked questions

## How do I buy in the store?

Add products to the cart, sign in, complete the shipping address and place
the order. Payment completes on the hosted Stripe page; the store never
receives or stores your card details.

## Which payment methods do you accept?

Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) via Stripe Checkout. You are
charged in the order currency.

## How long does shipping take?

- **Standard** shipping: **3 to 5 business days** after payment confirmation.
- **Express** shipping: **1 to 2 business days**, only for selected
  destinations. Every shipment includes a tracking number.

## How much does shipping cost?

Standard shipping is **free on orders over 50 USD**; below that amount it
costs **4.90 USD**. Express shipping costs **9.90 USD** where available.

## Can I return a product?

You have **30 calendar days** from delivery to return an unused product, with
accessories and original packaging. Refunds go to the original payment method
within **5 to 10 business days** after inspection.

## How do I track my order?

From your account, in the orders section, you will see the updated status. If
the order does not arrive within the stated window, contact the human support
team with the order number to open an incident.

## Do I need an account to buy?

You can browse and build the cart as a guest, but you must sign in to place
the order and see the order history.

## Who do I contact when the advisor cannot answer?

The advisor answers only from documents and catalog data. When it says it
does not have the information, contact the human support team: only they can
confirm specific cases (refunds, incidents, warranties).
