# Component compatibility FAQ

## Is this CPU compatible with my motherboard?

The **socket** must match (for example AM5 with AM5, LGA1700 with LGA1700)
along with the **chipset**. Each product spec sheet states its socket; when
they differ, the combination is incompatible whatever the price.

## Which RAM does my build need?

The **RAM type** must match the motherboard (**DDR4 with DDR4**,
**DDR5 with DDR5**); they are not interchangeable. Also check the maximum
capacity and supported speed in the spec sheet.

## Which power supply do I need for my graphics card?

Each card states its **power draw** and the **recommended supply** (for
example 300 W for a 70 W RTX 3050). Pick a supply with headroom over the
total system draw and enough PCIe connectors.

## Will my graphics card fit in my case?

Check the card **dimensions** (length, height and slot thickness) in its spec
sheet against the free space in the case, especially in compact or
low-profile cases.

## How do I know whether a laptop fits study or work?

Compare **RAM**, **storage**, **CPU** and **screen size** with your use:
office and study run on 8–16 GB of RAM and a 256–512 GB SSD; editing or
engineering usually want 16 GB or more and a stronger CPU.

## Can the advisor guarantee compatibility?

No. The advisor **explains** the outcome of the system compatibility rules
(socket, RAM type, power, dimensions); the human support team confirms the
final call on doubtful combinations.
