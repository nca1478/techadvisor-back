# How to claim the warranty

## Step 1: gather the information

Before writing, have the **order number**, the product **model and serial
number** and a **description of the fault** (when it happens and what you
tried) at hand.

## Step 2: contact human support

Write to the human support team with that data. The **automatic advisor can
never promise** repairs, replacements or refunds: only the human team
confirms whether the case is covered.

## Step 3: diagnosis

The team will ask for basic evidence (photos, error messages, steps to
reproduce the fault) and tell you whether the product must be sent to the
service center.

## Step 4: repair or replacement

When the case is covered, the product is **repaired or replaced**. When it is
not covered (misuse, out of term, exclusions), the team will offer the
available alternatives.

## Indicative times

The initial diagnosis is answered within **2 to 3 business days**. Repair
times depend on the service center and are confirmed case by case.
