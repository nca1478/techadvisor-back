# Warranty coverage

## Duration by category

- **Laptops** carry a **2-year warranty** from the delivery date.
- **Components** (motherboards, CPUs, memory, drives, graphics cards, power
  supplies) carry a **3-year warranty**.
- **Peripherals and accessories** (keyboards, mice, headphones, cables) carry
  a **1-year warranty**.

## What it covers

The warranty covers **manufacturing defects** and **hardware failures under
normal use**, including repair or replacement of the defective product. The
support team confirms whether the case is covered before authorizing any
repair.

## What it does not cover

The warranty does **not cover**: damage from misuse, drops or liquids;
unauthorized modifications; consumables with normal wear (for example
batteries); or third-party software.

## Proof needed

Keep the **order number** and the **delivery date**: they determine whether
the product is still inside its category warranty period.
