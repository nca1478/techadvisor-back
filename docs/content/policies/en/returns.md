# Returns policy

## Window

You have **30 calendar days** from delivery to return a product, no reason
required.

## Conditions

- The product must be **unused**, with its accessories and original packaging.
- Products with a **broken hygiene seal** (e.g. in-ear headphones) are only
  accepted when defective.

## Refunds

Once the product is received and inspected, the refund is issued to the
original payment method within **5 to 10 business days**. The assistant can
never promise refunds: only the human support team can confirm a specific
return.

## Exceptions

Any exception to this policy (extended windows, used products) requires
approval from the human support team. The assistant must hand these cases to
a person and must not promise the exception.
