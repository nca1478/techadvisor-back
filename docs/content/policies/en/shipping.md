# Shipping policy

## Delivery times

- **Standard** shipping: **3 to 5 business days** after payment confirmation.
- **Express** shipping: **1 to 2 business days**, available only for selected
  destinations.

## Costs

- Standard shipping is **free on orders over 50 USD**.
- Below that amount, standard shipping costs **4.90 USD**.
- Express shipping costs **9.90 USD** where available.

## Tracking

Every shipment includes a tracking number. If the order does not arrive
within the stated window, contact the human support team with the order
number to open an incident.
