# Payment methods

## Accepted methods

- **Credit and debit cards** (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) via Stripe.
- **Stripe Checkout**: payment completes on the Stripe-hosted page; the store
  never receives or stores card data.

## Currencies

Prices display in the store's configured supported currencies (ISO 4217 code
next to the amount). The charge runs in the order currency.

## Failed payments

If the payment fails or expires, the stock reservation is released
automatically and the order is cancelled. You may try again with a new order.
For apparent duplicate charges, contact the human support team: only they can
verify the charge status in Stripe.
