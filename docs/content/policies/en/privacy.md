# Privacy policy

## Data we process

We process the data needed for the purchase: email address, name, shipping
address and order history.

## Data the assistant never sees

The shopping assistant **never receives** your email, address, identity
document or payment data. It only receives your question, catalog data and
the retrieved document fragments.

## Your rights

You may request access, rectification or deletion of your data by contacting
the human support team. Assistant conversations are kept only to give
continuity to the advice.
