# Warranty policy

## Duration

- **Laptops** carry a **2-year warranty** from the delivery date.
- **Components** (motherboards, CPUs, memory, drives, graphics cards, power
  supplies) carry a **3-year warranty**.
- **Peripherals and accessories** (keyboards, mice, headphones, cables) carry
  a **1-year warranty**.

## Coverage

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal
use, including repair or replacement of the defective product.

## Exclusions

The warranty does **not cover**: damage from misuse, drops or liquids;
unauthorized modifications; consumables (normal battery wear); or
third-party software.

## How to claim

To claim the warranty, contact the human support team with the order number
and a description of the fault. The team will confirm whether the case is
covered before authorizing any repair or replacement.
